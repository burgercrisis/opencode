Apply a patch to modify multiple files. Supports adding, updating, and deleting files with context-aware changes.

Usage:
- The patch must be enclosed in markers:
  *** Begin Patch
  ...
  *** End Patch

- To add a file:
  *** Add File: <path>
  +<line 1>
  +<line 2>

- To delete a file:
  *** Delete File: <path>

- To update/move a file:
  *** Update File: <path>
  *** Move to: <new_path> (optional)
  @@ <context>
  - <old line>
  + <new line>
  *** End of File (optional)
